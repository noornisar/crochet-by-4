Store Policies & Terms

Custom & Bespoke Orders

At Crochet by 4, each custom item is passionately hand-knitted from scratch to fulfill your personal design dreams!

Important Custom Booking Guidelines:
  • How to Order: Simply send a Direct Message (DM) to our Instagram (@crochet_by_4) or message our WhatsApp at +92 313 7975149 with your desired design, color preferences, and sizing.
  • Advance Payment: A 50% advance deposit is strictly required before we purchase specific yarn and begin stitching your piece.
  • Early Booking: Handmade craft requires meticulous dedication. Please place your custom order at least 1 to 2 weeks early before your required date or event.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Because every crochet piece is custom handcrafted and cannot be restocked or reused once prepared, our policies are as follows:

  • No Refund & No Exchange: All handmade and custom orders are strictly final sale. We do not offer refunds, order cancellations after yarn preparation has begun, or product exchanges.
  • Order Confirmation: Once the 50% advance is paid, the order is locked into production and cannot be cancelled.
  • Transit Damage Protection: In the rare event an item arrives physically defective due to shipping, please contact us via WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery with an unboxing video.

Shipping & Delivery

We deliver our handcrafted crochet items nationwide across Pakistan directly to your doorstep.

  • Delivery Timeline: Once handmade crafting is completed (1–2 weeks), courier delivery typically takes 3 to 5 business days depending on your city.
  • Tracking: Tracking IDs and courier details will be shared with you via WhatsApp as soon as your parcel is dispatched.
  • Packaging: Each piece is wrapped securely with aesthetic boutique packaging to ensure it arrives in pristine condition.

Terms & Conditions

By placing an order on the Crochet by 4 website, Instagram, or WhatsApp, you agree to our standard handcrafted store terms:

  • Each product is 100% handmade; slight natural variations in stitch tightness, dimensions, or yarn dye lot colors add to the unique character of authentic crochet art.
  • Prices and lead times for custom orders are quoted on a per-project basis based on complexity and materials.
  • Customer is responsible for providing accurate delivery address and contact numbers.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. When you place an order or contact us:

  • We collect personal information (name, address, phone number, email) strictly to process and deliver your orders.
  • We will never sell, rent, or distribute your private contact details to any third-party marketing services.
  • Payment receipts and transaction proofs are kept secure and confidential.
